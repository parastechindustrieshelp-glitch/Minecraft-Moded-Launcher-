Welcome to Minecraft Launcher Setup!

Thank you for downloading Minecraft Launcher (Java Edition). This setup wizard will install the launcher and prepare all required game assets on your computer.

What this launcher provides:
  • Multi-version access (Release 1.5.2, 1.8.8, and 1.12.2).
  • Automatic offline local server runtime.
  • Isolated storage in %APPDATA% to protect your worlds and settings.

Installation Requirements:
  • Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit recommended).
  • Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime (installed automatically if missing).
  • At least 500 MB of free hard disk space.
